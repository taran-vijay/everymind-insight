How to read these results

  • Retrospective ratings. Participants recalled their earlier skill levels after training.
  • No control group. The survey describes self-reported change and associations.
  • Self-selection. People who responded may differ from those who skipped the survey or an item.
  • Stated intent. The calling item measures willingness to call 988.

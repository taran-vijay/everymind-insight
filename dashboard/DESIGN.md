# Training insights

This is a reading and decision-support surface for EveryMind training staff.
The page should make the evidence understandable before asking staff to act.

## Composition

- Compact top navigation keeps the report at the center of the screen.
- Display headings establish the topic; supporting copy uses a comfortable reading size.
- The overview has one wide chart surface. Recommendations and supporting charts use open sections rather than repeated cards.
- Two inline counts describe the report. Coverage and quality diagnostics live in their respective detail views.
- Neutral surfaces carry structure. Green identifies the current series and actionable links.
- A fixed 0–100% scale keeps agreement comparisons honest. One shared axis avoids repetition.
- Both series have direct numeric labels. Uncertainty is available from the chart control and in the detailed outcomes.

## Typography and behavior

Use platform fonts with optical sizing, size-specific tracking, and tabular numerals.
Keep important body copy at 15–19px; reserve 12–13px for bases, dates, legends, and footnotes.
Controls respond on press, without decorative page entrance animations.
Respect reduced motion, reduced transparency, and increased contrast preferences.
Charts stack their labels and values on narrow screens; the percentage scale never changes.

## Data rules

Keep the synthetic-data notice visible. Preserve response bases, confidence intervals,
suppression, and retrospective-rating limitations. Findings and actions come from the
report. Withhold them when any supporting metric is unavailable or suppressed.
Never infer hidden values, invent participant quotes, or send a selected report to a service.

## References

- [Apple Mac](https://www.apple.com/mac/): typographic hierarchy, restrained navigation, generous section rhythm.
- [Apple chart guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/charts): prominent data, fixed percentage scales, direct descriptions, accessible alternatives.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/features): clear focal points and supporting interface detail.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com): hierarchy through composition and typography.
- [Emil Kowalski](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills): immediate interaction feedback, good defaults, and consistent component details.

These references inform the craft; their branding and assets are not part of this project.
